# Hard Constraints

The non-negotiable rules for every tailored resume, cover letter, `resume-diff.md`, and application answer. They override the wiki rulebook (`knowledge/wiki/rulebook.md`) and every concept page. The `/tailor` and `/cover` skills load this file first.

Paths: engine scripts (`compile.sh`, `check_resume.py`, `scaffold.sh`) live in `engine/scripts/` of the plugin. `workspace/` is the user's own folder, in the directory Claude Code was started from, never the plugin install dir.

**Workspace rules** (apply to all three skills):

- `workspace/master-resume.tex` is the candidate's only source of truth and usually the only copy (it is gitignored, so git does not back it up). Never overwrite it during `/tailor` or `/cover`. Only `/setup` writes it, and only after a backup.
- `workspace/applications/<Company>/<Role>/` holds exactly `resume.tex`, `resume-diff.md`, `jd.md`, `resume.pdf` (plus `cover-letter.tex`/`cover-letter.pdf` after `/cover`). No `.aux`, `.log`, or `.out` there.
- Always compile with `compile.sh`, never raw `pdflatex`.

## The constraints

1. **One page max.** If it overflows, cut content — do not change `\documentclass` font size below `10pt` or margins below `0.4in`.
2. **Layout-strict**: single column only, no `multicol`, no two-column body layouts, no shaded/boxed content blocks. Use the custom template's existing `\resumeSubheading` / `\resumeProjectHeading` `tabular*` macros for date alignment. Keep FontAwesome contact icons, the navy section color, and the master's font setup from `workspace/master-resume.tex` — these are deliberate template exceptions to the wiki's generic no-icons/no-color ATS guidance (the master template wins); do not strip or restyle them for ATS unless the user asks. Compile with `compile.sh <Company> <Role>` (scratch in `workspace/build/<Company>/<Role>/`, final PDF placed at `workspace/applications/<Company>/<Role>/resume.pdf`, mechanical constraint checks run automatically).
3. **Truthful**: never fabricate employers, dates, degrees, metrics, or tools. You may *rephrase*, *reorder*, *re-emphasize*, *re-quantify with numbers the user already provided*, and *promote/demote* existing content. You may **not** invent new projects or numbers. Bullet *counts* are set by Hard Constraint 11, and redistributing a role's existing facts across a different number of bullets is expected, not fabrication. If a number is missing, ask before adding. **Mechanically enforced**: `check_resume.py` extracts every numeric token from each tailored bullet and fails the build unless that token also appears in master — scoped per employer in Experience, so a figure can neither drift (`97.3\%` becoming `97\%`) nor migrate between roles, and scoped per section elsewhere. Rewriting prose freely is expected. The figures must survive byte-for-byte. The checker also fails any employer, school, role, or project heading (`\resumeSubheading`, `\resumeSubSubheading`, `\resumeProjectHeading`) that isn't copied unchanged from master, so an entry can't be invented or relabeled.
4. **Bullets use XYZ + strong action verbs**: `Accomplished [X] as measured by [Y] by doing [Z]`. Start with a past-tense action verb (Led, Built, Designed, Negotiated, Reduced, Launched, Trained, Coordinated, Analyzed...). **Each opener must be unique on the page** (no duplicate opening verbs across Experience/Projects/Awards bullets) and must match the bullet's **primary action** (context-first from the verb inventories, not a generic synonym swap). Lead with quantified impact when available (latency, accuracy, revenue, users, $, %, ×, ms, p95). No "responsible for", no "helped with", no first-person pronouns. See `knowledge/wiki/concepts/action-verbs.md` Rules 2-3 (context-first and unique openers).
5. **No em dashes**: Never use the em dash character (`—`) in tailored resume bullets, `resume-diff.md`, application-question answers, cover letters, or user-facing prose. Use commas, periods, or parentheses instead. Example: write "I owned the launch, from budget to final report" not "I owned the launch — from budget to final report."
6. **No semicolons or colons**: Never use semicolons (`;`) or colons (`:`) as sentence connectors in tailored resume bullets, `resume-diff.md`, application-question answers, cover letters, or user-facing prose. Split into two sentences or use commas instead. Example: write "I rebuilt the intake process. It cut wait times by half." not "I rebuilt the intake process; it cut wait times by half." Example: write "The model used three inputs, price, volume, and volatility." not "The model used three inputs: price, volume, and volatility." This does **not** apply to colons required by template syntax: the Skills block's `\textbf{Category}{: ...}` format, the "Relevant Coursework:" label, and `tel:`/`mailto:` link schemes in the contact line all keep their colons as-is.
7. **Keep all Experience titles**: Every employer/role from `workspace/master-resume.tex` MUST appear in the tailored Experience section with the same company name, job title, dates, and location. You may reorder bullets within a role and rewrite bullets, with per-entry bullet counts set by Hard Constraint 11, but **never omit an entire experience entry**. **Entries stay reverse-chronological** (newest end date first, ties broken by newest start date), exactly as in master: never reorder whole Experience or Education entries to put a more JD-relevant role on top. Relevance is expressed through bullet content and counts, not entry order. **Mechanically enforced**: `check_resume.py` parses each subheading's dates and fails the build when Experience or Education is out of order.
8. **Do not alter template spacing**: Copy the master preamble and custom commands **verbatim**. Never change line spacing, vertical gaps, or list spacing in the template — no edits to `\vspace`, `\itemsep`, `\parsep`, `\topsep`, margins, `\addtolength`, `\titleformat` spacing, or `\resumeItem` / `\resumeSubheading` / `\resumeProjectHeading` vertical tweaks. If the resume overflows one page, cut **content** (bullets, projects, coursework) per One-Page Discipline — not layout hacks. Master's spacing is deliberately tuned: reproduce it exactly, and `check_resume.py` flags any `\vspace` value master's body doesn't use.
9. **Never browse `applications/` output**: `workspace/applications/` is write-once output storage, not reference material. Do not `ls`, `find`, glob, or read any `applications/<Company>/<Role>/` folder other than the exact one you are currently working on or one the user explicitly named. Never skim past resumes, diffs, JDs, or cover letters for phrasing, keywords, structure, or template code — `workspace/master-resume.tex` and the wiki are the only permitted sources. If the target Company/Role is ambiguous, ask the user directly instead of enumerating the directory to guess. The only exceptions are the user explicitly requesting a cross-folder operation (e.g., "fix the Acme resume", a batch audit) and the engine scripts operating on their named target folder. **Mechanically enforced** in Claude Code by the plugin's PreToolUse hook (`hooks/guard_workspace.py`, registered in `hooks/hooks.json`): any Bash/Read/Write/Edit/Glob/Grep call referencing `applications/` is blocked unless every reference is a concrete wildcard-free `applications/<Company>/<Role>/` path. Cross-folder batch work must go through a script in `engine/scripts/`.
10. **Line-fill discipline (no orphan tails)**: a full body line in the compiled PDF fits roughly 110-115 characters (measured ≈114 on the master template) — use that estimate while drafting bullet lengths. In Experience and Projects, a bullet must never wrap so that its last rendered line holds only a few stray words (2-5 words spilling onto a new line, leaving the rest of that line as wasted white space). While drafting or rewriting any bullet, target lengths that land near full lines (about 1.0, or 1.7-2.0 rendered lines). If a draft bullet spills a short tail, either **trim** wording back to the previous line boundary or **expand** the bullet with real, master-supported specifics (stack, scale, metric) until the last line is at least ~40% full: never expand by inventing content (Constraint 3). Education coursework and Skills lines are mildly exempt, but still avoid tails there when it is easy. Shipping an orphan tail in Experience/Projects is acceptable **only** after genuine trim and expand attempts both fail without violating truthfulness or other constraints, and `resume-diff.md` must note that this was attempted. **Not mechanically enforced**: `check_resume.py` does not check line fill — apply this rule by eyeballing the compiled PDF yourself. **Sequencing**: deal with line-fill fixes only at the end, after the one-page constraint is satisfied — do not chase them mid-cut, since content cuts reshuffle line breaks. Once the resume fits one page, check the PDF and fix any remaining orphan tails before delivering.
11. **Bullet count allocation (per-entry caps)**: bullet counts are a planning decision made during /tailor step 3, not something discovered while cutting.
    - **Experience**: an internship, externship, co-op, or trainee role (title matching `Intern`, `Internship`, `Extern`, `Externship`, `Co-op`, or `Trainee`) gets **exactly 1** bullet. Every other role gets **2 or 3**, never more than **3**. Allocate upward by the weight of the role, combining duration, seniority, and relevance to this JD, so the longest-running and most JD-relevant role carries 3 while a shorter or more peripheral role carries 2. Every entry keeps at least 1 bullet (Hard Constraint 7 keeps the entry itself on the page). Only an overflow that survives the One-Page Discipline cut order may take a non-internship role down to 1.
    - **Projects**: **1** bullet per project by default, **2** maximum. A project earns its second bullet only when one bullet cannot carry the JD-relevant substance, or when spare budget lines remain after Experience is allocated.
    - **Spend leftover space on project depth**: when the step 3 projection lands under the 52-line budget, promote projects from 1 bullet to 2 (highest JD relevance first) until the budget is full. This is the inverse of the One-Page Discipline cut order and runs at the same point, during step 3 sizing, never as a post-compile patch.
    - **Mechanically enforced**: `check_resume.py` fails the build when an Experience role carries more than 3 bullets (each `\resumeSubSubheading` role under one employer counts on its own), an internship-titled role carries more than 1, a project carries more than 2, or any entry carries none. The soft defaults (1 project bullet, the 2-vs-3 experience ladder) are judgment calls, not checked.
12. **Compose fresh, never reword master**: every tailored Experience/Projects bullet is written new from the /tailor step 2.5 fact inventory plus the JD's vocabulary, never by editing master's sentence. Keep master's numbers and proper nouns byte-for-byte (Constraint 3), but change the opening verb, the lead fact, and the clause order to fit this JD. A bullet that reads like master with a few words swapped is a failed tailor, even when master's version already fits the JD. Before posting the step 3 diff, check each AFTER against its BEFORE: different verb (unless the JD names that exact verb), different lead fact or angle, and no clause lifted from master (e.g., a phrase like "validated against a held-out ground-truth set" carried over word for word). **Mechanically enforced**: `check_resume.py` fails the build when a bullet shares 9+ consecutive words with, or is 60%+ similar to, a master bullet in the same scope (same employer, or Projects), and warns when it reuses the source bullet's opening verb. The checker only runs after files are written, so meet this rule while drafting, not at compile time.
