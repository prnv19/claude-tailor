# The XYZ Formula

> **Raw source**: `../../raw/xyz-formula.md` (research notes)
> **Author / origin**: Laszlo Bock, then SVP of People Operations at Google. LinkedIn Influencer post.
> **Date published / accessed**: Published 2014-09-29; accessed 2026-10-01.
> **URL**: https://www.calstatela.edu/sites/default/files/formula_for_a_winning_resume.docx (copy of the LinkedIn post)

## Key highlights
1. **The primary source for XYZ.** "Accomplished [X] as measured by [Y] by doing [Z]." Every other guide in the wiki quotes it second-hand.
2. **The formula has four moves, not three**: an active verb, a number, a **baseline or comparison** that makes the number meaningful, and how it was done.
3. **Anticipate "is that a big deal?"** Adding "$1.2M" to "12%" answers the reviewer's next question before they ask it. A percentage alone is half a claim.
4. **Say how, not just what.** The method clause "adds credibility and gives insight into your strengths". It is why the strongest rewrite of each example adds a "by doing" clause.
5. **Even skills claims take proof.** "Excellent customer service skills" becomes credible only "as demonstrated by" a concrete result.
6. **Selectivity is a number.** "One of three officers selected" quantifies a bullet with no metric.
7. **Everything is measurable relative to peers.** A comparison to the team average turns a modest number into a differentiator.

## Unique contributions
- Only source that adds **baseline/comparison** as an explicit step of the formula. Later guides drop it, which is why so many "XYZ" bullets carry naked percentages.
- Only source with a **multi-number decomposition** example: one headline saving split into the drivers that produced it.
- First-hand authority: the formula's originator, writing as Google's head of people operations.

## Key claims
- The resume's "sole purpose" is to get past the first screen into an interview.
- Baseline polish (concise, error-free, legible, honest) is assumed before the formula even applies.
- Specificity wins even for small jobs: "Recruiters will nevertheless love the specificity."
- "Providing data helps. Making it meaningful with a comparison helps even more."

## Direct quotes worth reusing
- *"Accomplished [X] as measured by [Y] by doing [Z]"*
- *"Start with an active verb, numerically measure what you accomplished, provide a baseline for comparison, and detail what you did to achieve your goal."*
- *"Adding '($1.2M)' anticipates the reviewer's question about whether 12% is a big deal or not."*
- *"Providing data helps. Making it meaningful with a comparison helps even more."*

## Caveats & re-ingest notes
- 2014, written for all professions, not tech specifically. The structure has aged well. The examples are finance, sales, and logistics flavored.
- The formula is about content, not order: the rendered sentence may lead with the verb and outcome and put the method last.
