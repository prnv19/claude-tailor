# Page Length Study

> **Raw source**: `../../raw/page-length-study.md` (research notes)
> **Author / origin**: ResumeGo (resume-writing vendor). Hiring simulation with 482 recruiters, hiring managers, HR staff, and executives.
> **Date published / accessed**: Published 2018-12; accessed 2026-10-01.
> **URL**: https://www.resumego.net/research/one-or-two-page-resumes/

## Key highlights
1. **Recruiters picked two-page resumes 2.3x as often** as matched one-page versions in a hiring simulation.
2. **The preference grows with seniority**: 1.4x entry-level, 2.6x mid-level, 2.9x managerial.
3. **Two-page resumes scored 21% higher** (8.6 vs 7.1) and were read longer (4 min 5 s vs 2 min 24 s).
4. **The confound is content, not just length**: the two-page versions carried about twice the words (700-850 vs 350-500), so the study can't separate "longer" from "more detailed".
5. **The strongest counter-evidence to a universal one-page rule**, especially for experienced candidates.

## Unique contributions
- Only source in the wiki that **argues against one page** with data instead of opinion.
- Splits results by **job level**, the dimension every other guide uses informally ("one page unless senior").

## Key claims
- 5,375 of 7,712 chosen resumes were two pages.
- Participants may be "unaware of their own tendency of favoring longer resumes" (authors' caveat).

## Direct quotes worth reusing
- *"Recruiters were 2.3 times as likely to prefer two-page resumes over one-page resumes."*

## Where it feeds the wiki
- [`../concepts/one-page-discipline.md`](../concepts/one-page-discipline.md) Disagreements: noted, not adopted. The pipeline stays one page, and this is the evidence for an optional two-page mode for experienced candidates later.

## Disagreements
- **With every guide in the wiki** (`ml-engineer-resume-guide.md` §6, `resume-as-a-product.md`, `ats-friendly-resume-guide.md`, `data-science-resume-walkthrough.md`), which default to one page below senior level. **Resolution**: one page stays the default and a hard constraint of this pipeline. The simulation confounds length with content, and its entry-level effect (1.4x) is its weakest. The 10+ years threshold from `data-science-resume-walkthrough.md` remains the point where two pages become defensible.

## Caveats & re-ingest notes
- Vendor research (ResumeGo sells resume writing) in a simulated, not live, hiring setting.
- 2018 data, before the post-2022 surge in application volume, which favors fast skimming.
